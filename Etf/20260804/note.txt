What is intelligence?
================================
Intelligence means a system or ability to acquire and apply knowledge and skills.
It can also refer to the capacity for learning, reasoning, understanding, and problem-solving.

what is ai?
================================
AI, or Artificial Intelligence, refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think and learn like humans. It encompasses a variety of technologies and methods that enable machines to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.
    1. Acting Humanly
        - Turing Test ( Alan Turing, 1950 )
            * A test to determine whether a machine can exhibit intelligent behavior indistinguishable from that of a human.
    
    2. Thinking Humanly
        - How Human Think ?
            Introspection: The process of examining one's own thoughts and feelings.
            Psychology: The scientific study of the mind and behavior, which helps in understanding how humans think and learn.
            Brain imaging: observing brain activities.

        - Cognitive Science: An interdisciplinary field that studies the mind and its processes, including 
          how humans think, learn, and remember.
    
    3. Act Rationally
        simple meaning is act the correct way to achieve the goal.
        - Rational Agent: An entity that perceives its environment and takes actions to maximize its chances of achieving its goals.
        - Rationality: The quality of being based on or in accordance with reason or logic.

    4. Think Rationally
        simple meaning is think the correct way to achieve the goal.
        - Logic: The study of reasoning and argumentation, which helps in developing algorithms and models for AI systems.
        - Reasoning: The process of drawing conclusions from available information, which is essential for decision-making in AI.
            * Inductive Reasoning: A method of reasoning in which generalizations are made based on specific observations or examples, 
              often used in machine learning to identify patterns and make predictions.
            * Deductive Reasoning: A method of reasoning in which conclusions are drawn from general principles or premises,
              often used in rule-based AI systems to derive specific outcomes from established rules.
